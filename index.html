Why Vera

Other scanners guess. Vera shows its work.

Most food scanners run your product through an AI that gives a different answer if you ask twice. Vera runs on fixed, published rules — so the score means something.

Deterministic

Same ingredient, same rating — every time. Fixed rules, not an AI that contradicts itself between scans.

Cited

Every flag links to a published study you can check yourself — not vibes, not a mystery algorithm.

Private

Your scans stay on your device. No account, no ads, no brand money, and we never sell your data.

How it works

Seconds from shelf to verdict.

STEP 01

Scan the barcode

Point your camera at any packaged food. No barcode? Snap the ingredients and Vera reads them on your device.

STEP 02

Read the verdict

A 0–100 score, seed-oil and processing flags, and an ingredient-by-ingredient breakdown — each one cited.

STEP 03

Swap it

See cleaner, higher-scoring picks in the same category, and build a shopping list from them.

What's inside

Built for people who read the back of the box.

Seed-oil detection

Flags refined seed oils under every name they hide behind — canola, "vegetable oil," and the rest.

Ingredient breakdown

Every ingredient rated good, neutral or bad — with a one-line, always-identical reason.

Cleaner alternatives Pro

See meaningfully cleaner products in the same category, and add them to a shopping list.

Shareable score cards

Save a clean, branded card of any product's score to your camera roll to share or post.

Your Clean Score over time Pro

One number that tracks how your scans trend, so you can see whether the shopping cart is actually changing.

Built on Open Food Facts

An open, community-built food database of millions of products — plus Open Beauty Facts for personal care.

Pricing

Scanning is free. Always.

Vera Pro
