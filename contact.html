Who you're writing to

Nathaniel Fiskå — sole trader, based in Norway. I build Vera, write the scoring rules, and answer the email.

What to write about

Something in the app is broken

Include your iPhone model and iOS version if you can. A screenshot usually saves a round trip.

A product's data is wrong

Send the barcode. The app's verdict screen also has a "report a data issue" link that fills the barcode in for you.

You disagree with a score

Genuinely welcome. Tell me the ingredient and why. Every rule Vera applies is published with the research behind it, and I'd rather be corrected than consistent-and-wrong.

Billing, refunds or restoring a purchase

Check Support first — most of these are handled by Apple rather than by me, and it's faster. If it's still stuck, write.

Privacy requests

Deletion or questions about data. See the Privacy Policy for what's actually held — it's very little, and there are no accounts.

Press, partnerships and creators

Same address. Note that no brand can pay for a better score, or for placement — that isn't a negotiating position, it's the product.

What I can't help with

  • Refunds. Apple processes every payment, so refunds go through reportaproblem.apple.com. I have no ability to issue one.
  • Medical or dietary advice. I'm not qualified to give it, and Vera isn't either. Please talk to a professional.
  • Confirming a product is safe for an allergy. Never rely on Vera or on me for that — read the physical packaging.
Vera's scores are an information tool, not medical advice. Always check the physical packaging for allergen information — only the label in your hand is authoritative.
