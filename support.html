Subscriptions & billing

How do I cancel Vera Pro?

Vera Pro is billed by Apple, so it is cancelled in iOS Settings — not inside the app:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name at the very top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap Vera, then Cancel Subscription.

You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. If you cancel during a free trial, you are not charged — but do it at least 24 hours before the trial ends, because Apple bills at the moment the trial expires.

I paid but the app still says Free

Open the paywall in the app and tap Restore. That re-checks your Apple ID's purchases. Two things to confirm first: you are signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with, and the device has a working internet connection. If Restore still doesn't do it, email us with the date of purchase and we'll sort it out.

I want a refund

All Vera purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds go through Apple rather than us — we have no ability to issue one. Request it at reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple ID you purchased with. If the reason is that Vera did something wrong, email us too, so we can fix it.

I have a creator code

Open Settings → Redeem a creator code in the app, or tap "Have a creator code?" on the trial screen during onboarding. Codes are not case-sensitive.

Scanning

My product isn't found

Vera looks products up in Open Food Facts, an open database built by volunteers. It is very large but not complete, and coverage varies by country. If your product isn't there, the verdict screen has a link to add it — that takes a couple of minutes and means the next person who scans it gets a real score. You can also try the app's manual product search.

The score looks wrong

Two different things can cause this, and it's worth telling them apart:

  • The ingredients are wrong or missing. That's the underlying database, and it's fixable — the verdict screen has a "report a data issue" link that emails us with the barcode attached.
  • The ingredients are right but you disagree with the rating. That's our rules, and we want to hear it. Every rule in Vera is published with the research behind it, in the app's research section. Email us the ingredient and what you think we got wrong.

The camera won't scan

Check that Vera has camera access in Settings → Vera → Camera. Barcodes read best in even light, with the whole code in frame and the phone held still for a moment. If the barcode is damaged or curved, use the app's manual search instead.

Why did the same product score differently in another app?

Because most scanners score differently on purpose. Vera weights industrial seed oils, additives and degree of processing heavily, and it applies fixed rules rather than asking an AI — so Vera gives the same product the same score every time, and shows you every ingredient that moved it. Whether you agree with those weightings is a fair question, and the research behind each one is in the app.

Your data

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings → Reset everything in the app. That erases your profile, scan history, saved items and lists immediately and permanently. Deleting the app does the same. There is no account, so there's nothing left on a server afterwards.

What do you collect?

Very little, and never your name or email — there are no accounts. The full detail, service by service, is in the Privacy Policy.
